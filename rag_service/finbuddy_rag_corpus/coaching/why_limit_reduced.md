# Why was my limit reduced?

If your approved amount is lower than you expected, or lower than a
previous offer, it's almost always because of a change in one or two of
the same 8 signals FinBuddy always looks at — not a black-box decision,
and not personal.

## The most common reasons, in plain terms

- **Your income looked less regular this time.** If your monthly income
  swings up and down more than before, that reads as higher risk, even if
  your average income is the same or higher.
- **Your balance dipped low more often.** Frequent low-balance moments
  suggest less of a cushion to handle a bad month, which affects how much
  we can responsibly offer.
- **Fewer months of data, or a gap in your data.** A shorter or
  interrupted Account Aggregator consent window gives us less to go on,
  so we tend to be more conservative.
- **Your transaction pattern narrowed.** Working with fewer
  platforms/customers than before, or a drop in transaction count, can
  look like reduced activity even if it isn't.

## How to see exactly why, for your specific decision

Every FinBuddy score comes with your **top 3 specific factors** — the
actual signals that moved your score, in plain language, not a generic
list like the one above. If you're not sure which of these applied to
you, ask "why was my limit reduced" again after checking your latest
score result, and the coach will reference your specific top factors, not
just the general list.

## What you can do about it

Because the factors are specific and visible, they're also actionable:

- If income regularity was the issue, try to get at least one predictable
  income credit landing on consistent days each month.
- If balance dips were the issue, building even a small buffer (a few
  hundred rupees) can help.
- If it was about data history, sharing a longer consent window (more
  months of UPI history) gives us more to work with next time.

FinBuddy's roadmap feature is built around exactly this: concrete,
achievable steps tied to your specific factors, with a sense of how much
they could move your next offer.
