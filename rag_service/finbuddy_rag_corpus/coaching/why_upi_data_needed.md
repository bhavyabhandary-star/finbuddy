# Why does FinBuddy need my UPI data?

Most gig workers don't have a traditional credit history — no long
relationship with a bank, no existing loans to show a track record. But
your UPI transactions already tell a real story about your income: how
often money comes in, how steady it is, how many different platforms or
customers you work with, and how you manage your balance month to month.

FinBuddy reads that story instead of asking you to have a credit history
you were never given the chance to build.

## What we actually look at

Specifically, we look at 8 signals from your UPI activity over up to the
last 12 months: your average monthly income, how regular that income is,
how many transactions you make, how many different people/platforms you
transact with, how often your balance runs low, how much of your income
looks like business income versus personal payments, your typical
transaction size, and how many months of history you've shared.

We do **not** see your bank password, your PIN, or the ability to move
money — only transaction history, shared through your bank's own secure
Account Aggregator consent flow, for the specific purpose of scoring your
loan application.

## Why we ask you to actively consent, every time

Under India's data protection rules, we're only allowed to use your data
for the specific purpose you agreed to, and you have to actively opt in —
we can't pre-check a box for you, and we can't bundle this into some other
agreement. If FinBuddy ever wants to use your data for something new, we
have to ask you again, specifically, for that new purpose.

## You're in control

You can review what you've consented to, and you can withdraw consent,
through the same Account Aggregator flow you used to share it. If you
withdraw consent, we stop pulling new data — it doesn't retroactively
undo a lending decision already made on data you'd previously shared, but
it does stop any further access going forward.
