# I'm having trouble repaying my loan — what do I do?

If a repayment is coming up and you're not going to make it, or you've
already missed one, the most useful thing you can do is say so before it
becomes a pattern — not disappear and hope it resolves itself.

## What actually happens if you're struggling

Missing a single payment doesn't erase your progress. It does show up in
your ongoing UPI activity picture, and repeated or unexplained missed
payments will affect your future limit and terms, the same way any of the
other 8 signals would. Reaching out early, before a missed payment, keeps
more options open than reaching out after several.

## What to do right now

- **Check your repayment schedule** in the app before assuming what's due
  and when.
- **If you know you'll be short**, ask about your options before the due
  date, not after — some arrangements are easier to make proactively.
- **If you've already missed a payment**, don't let it compound silently.
  A single missed payment you address quickly reads very differently from
  a pattern of missed payments.
- **Keep transacting through your linked UPI account** even during a
  tough month — a temporary dip in income is a very different signal than
  an account going quiet.

## Remember what FinBuddy can and can't do here

FinBuddy is the scoring and coaching layer, not the lender you owe money
to — repayment logistics (rescheduling, hardship terms) are ultimately the
Regulated Entity's (bank/NBFC's) decision, per how these lending
partnerships are structured. What FinBuddy can do is help you understand
how your repayment behavior affects your score and coach you toward
options — it's the right first place to ask "what happens if I miss a
payment" or "how do I get back on track," even when the final approval on
a repayment change sits with the lender.
